HALCYON PRODUCT SPECIFICATIONS, 2026 CATALOG

Stratus 2 Ultralight Tent
The Stratus 2 is a two-person, three-season backpacking tent. Its packed weight is
1.18 kg (2 lb 10 oz). The floor area is 2.7 square metres with two doors and two
vestibules. The poles are made from DAC Featherlite aluminium and the fly is
20-denier silicone-coated ripstop nylon with a 1,500 mm hydrostatic head. Setup
time for a first-time user is about 6 minutes. Retail price: $429.

Cirrus 55 Backpack
The Cirrus 55 is a 55-litre pack for multi-day trips. It weighs 1.05 kg and is
rated for loads up to 18 kg. The frame uses a removable carbon fibre stay. The hip
belt has two zippered pockets and the pack comes in three torso sizes: small
(38-43 cm), medium (43-48 cm) and large (48-53 cm). Retail price: $279.

Nimbus 15 Sleeping Bag
The Nimbus 15 is a mummy bag filled with 850-fill-power responsibly sourced goose
down. Its comfort rating is -4 C and its lower limit rating is -9 C. It weighs
890 g and compresses to the size of a 5-litre stuff sack. Retail price: $389.

Ember Camp Stove
The Ember is a canister stove that boils 1 litre of water in 3 minutes 20 seconds
at sea level. It weighs 83 g and has a piezo igniter. It is compatible with
standard EN417 threaded fuel canisters. Retail price: $64.
