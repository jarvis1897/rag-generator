# Halcyon Outdoor Gear: Returns and Warranty Policy

## Standard returns

Unused items can be returned within 45 days of delivery for a full refund to the
original payment method. Items must be in their original packaging with all tags
attached. Refunds are issued within 7 business days after the return is inspected
at our Reno, Nevada warehouse.

Return shipping is free for members of the Halcyon Trailhead loyalty program.
Everyone else pays a flat return shipping fee of $8.50, which is deducted from the
refund.

## Used gear

Gear that has been used outdoors can be returned within 30 days if it has a
manufacturing defect. Used gear without a defect is not eligible for a refund, but
it can be traded in through the Second Summit program for store credit worth up to
40% of the original price.

## Warranty

All Halcyon-branded tents and backpacks carry a 10-year limited warranty against
defects in materials and workmanship. Sleeping bags carry a 5-year warranty.
Damage from normal wear and tear, UV exposure, animals, or improper storage is not
covered.

To file a warranty claim, email warranty@halcyon.example with your order number and
photos of the defect. Claims are reviewed within 10 business days. If approved, we
repair the item first; if a repair is not possible, we replace it with the same or
an equivalent model.

## Exceptions

Gift cards, personalized items with custom embroidery, and opened fuel canisters
cannot be returned.
